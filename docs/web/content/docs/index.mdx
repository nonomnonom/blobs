title: Hello, Blobs.
description: A little help with your on-device research companion.
---

Blobs answers questions with a local language model and the libraries saved on
your phone, then opens the source passages behind an answer. The 0.5.0 release
runs this flow on a physical Android phone with airplane mode on.

There is no account flow, and the app does not declare a Google Play Services
dependency, which is what a GrapheneOS install requires. It has not been run on
a GrapheneOS build.

## New here?

Start with [installation and setup](/docs/get-started). You’ll choose a model and can add the starter library when you want local sources; a model is enough for your first conversation.

Prefer a look around first? [Take the screen tour](/docs/tour) to see setup, chat, sources, libraries, and settings in the Android app.

## Make it useful for you

- [Chat and sources](/docs/chat): ask questions, follow citations, and return to conversations.
- [Your library](/docs/library): keep travel guides, reference, repair know-how, and everyday knowledge nearby.
- [Your own files](/docs/your-files): bring in documents from your phone.
- [Models](/docs/models): understand the choices and change what runs on your device.
- [Privacy and local-use design](/docs/privacy): see what stays on your phone and when the app connects.

## Building or contributing?

The [developer guide](/docs/developers) covers the Android build. Read about the [architecture](/docs/architecture), [library pipeline](/docs/corpus), or [contributing](/docs/contributing).

These guides describe the 0.5.0 release and its bundled download list. A newer app release or a user-requested list update can add more choices. Downloads are available on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/nonomnonom/blobs/releases/latest).
