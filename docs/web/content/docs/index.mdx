Blobs is an Android app that answers questions using a language model and a library saved on your phone. Open the sources behind an answer and keep reading, even without a connection.

There’s no account to create. Blobs is compatible with GrapheneOS and doesn’t depend on Google Play Services.

## New here?

Start with [installation and setup](/docs/get-started). You’ll choose a model, download the starter library, and be ready for your first conversation.

Prefer a look around first? [Take the screen tour](/docs/tour) to see setup, chat, sources, libraries, and settings in the Android app.

## Make it useful for you

- [Chat and sources](/docs/chat): ask questions, follow citations, and return to conversations.
- [Your library](/docs/library): keep travel guides, outdoor reference, and everyday knowledge nearby.
- [Your own files](/docs/your-files): bring in documents from your phone.
- [Models](/docs/models): understand the choices and change what runs on your device.
- [Privacy and offline use](/docs/privacy): see what stays on your phone and when the app connects.

## Building or contributing?

The [developer guide](/docs/developers) covers the Android build. Read about the [architecture](/docs/architecture), [library pipeline](/docs/corpus), or [contributing](/docs/contributing).

These guides describe the current source and bundled download list. A newer app release or a user-requested list update can add more choices. Downloads are available on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/nonomnonom/blobs/releases/latest).
