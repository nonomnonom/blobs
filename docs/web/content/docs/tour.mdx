These screens come from the 0.4.0 build on an Android 16 emulator, in Light
appearance. The 0.5.0 release, which is what you download today, runs the same
flow on a physical Android phone. Screens show the interface; model
recommendations, storage, and saved collections depend on your phone. Tap a
screenshot to see it at a comfortable reading size.

## A gentle start

Choose a model that fits, then head into chat. Add the starter library or other collections when you want local sources.

    names={['welcome', 'setup-model', 'setup-library', 'setup-ready']}

Follow the [setup guide](/docs/get-started) for each step.

## Your next question

The composer keeps your model and source selection close to the question. When you want to look beyond an answer, open its numbered citation and read the source inside Blobs.

<ScreenGallery names={['home', 'reader']} />

Learn about [chat and citations](/docs/chat). The source shown here is [’s-Hertogenbosch, from Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/%27s-Hertogenbosch), under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); its contributor history and source credits remain with the article.

## A library you choose

**Your collections** holds saved material. **Discover more** is where you find additional libraries. Read the contents, language, size, and source details before downloading.

<ScreenGallery names={['library', 'discover']} />

See [what’s in each library](/docs/library).

## Your phone, your preferences

Change models, follow Android’s text-size preferences, or choose an appearance that feels comfortable. Storage and conversation controls stay together in Settings.
