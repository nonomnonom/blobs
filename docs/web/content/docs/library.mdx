Open **Library** to browse saved material or discover more. Read a pack’s contents, language, source, edition, and download size before choosing it.

## Saved and available

Downloading a library saves its articles on the phone. Enabling it makes those articles available when Blobs searches for sources for an answer. You can turn a saved library off without deleting it, or remove it to reclaim storage.

Search and open saved articles directly in the app. To keep a conversation focused, select the particular sources you want to use.

## What can I download?

The bundled list has a starter travel library and optional collections covering Wikipedia, repair and fixing, practical how-to, history and primary texts, and named places. All current packs are in English.

## What each collection holds

**Wikivoyage English travel guides** is the starter and the broad destination collection. It is a snapshot, not an offline map or live travel service. Fares, entry requirements, opening hours, and local conditions can change after the snapshot.

**Wikipedia English Top 1M introductions** holds the lead sections of the most-read English articles. It is introductions only, so a full article body is not in the pack.

**Repair and fixing** holds community questions and answers about vehicles, bicycles, aviation, 3D printing, and woodworking. **Practical how-to** covers cooking, personal finance, travel, and home improvement. Answers are community-written, not guaranteed correct or current, and safety-critical repair must be checked against current manufacturer documentation.

**History and primary texts** is one reviewed Wikisource edition split across four volumes: treaties, constitutions, court opinions, speeches, letters, and published works. The split is deterministic by document hash, not by subject, so install all four volumes to hold the complete edition. Transcriptions are not critical editions, and laws and court opinions can be amended or superseded.

**Nairobi health places** lists 200 named clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies in central Nairobi from an OpenStreetMap snapshot. It does not report whether a place is open, how far it is, or how good it is.

## Check for new choices

When connected, tap **Check for updates**. Blobs fetches and verifies the latest download list. It doesn’t automatically download new packs or replace your saved files.

The bundled or last verified list is stored locally. Each download starts only
after you choose it. A failed download leaves the existing library in place.

## Sources and editions

Open a library’s details and article credits to see its origin, edition, and license. Saved libraries are snapshots rather than live feeds. Use current local instructions for emergencies and check time-sensitive travel details before setting out.
