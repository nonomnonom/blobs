Open **Library** to browse saved material or discover more. Read a pack’s contents, language, source, edition, and download size before choosing it.

## Saved and available

Downloading a library saves its articles on the phone. Enabling it makes those articles available when Blobs searches for sources for an answer. You can turn a saved library off without deleting it, or remove it to reclaim storage.

Search and open saved articles directly in the app. To keep a conversation focused, select the particular sources you want to use.

## What can I download?

The bundled list has a starter library and optional collections covering Wikipedia, travel, outdoor safety, emergency preparedness, and practical living. All current packs are in English.

## Travel and practical living

**Travel guides · English** is the broad destination collection. **Bali travel guides** is a small, 30-article selection from the same September 2026 material, useful when you only need that region. Installing both does not add new Bali articles. Blobs avoids repeating identical passages in retrieval.

These are text guides about places and getting around. They don’t contain offline maps, GPS navigation, route calculation, or live transport information. Fares, entry requirements, opening hours, and local conditions can change after the snapshot.

**Practical living** contains four Appropedia articles about composting, drip irrigation, rainwater collection, and solar cooking. It is a small text reference collection without the source diagrams or videos, rather than a complete construction or water-treatment manual.

## Check for new choices

When connected, tap **Check for updates**. Blobs fetches and verifies the latest download list. It doesn’t automatically download new packs or replace your saved files.

The bundled or last verified list remains available offline. Each download starts only after you choose it. Existing libraries keep working if a download fails.

## Sources and editions

Open a library’s details and article credits to see its origin, edition, and license. Saved libraries are snapshots rather than live feeds. Use current local instructions for emergencies and check time-sensitive travel details before setting out.
