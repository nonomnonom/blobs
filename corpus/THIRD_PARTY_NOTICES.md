See [selectolax](https://github.com/rushter/selectolax) and [Lexbor](https://github.com/lexbor/lexbor) for upstream source and notices. Development environments and third-party wheels are not included in source bundles; the lock file recreates them.

Wikipedia text remains attributed to Wikipedia contributors under the applicable [CC BY-SA 4.0 text](licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt). Blobs extracts/reformats text, removes page navigation and images, and converts tables to text rows. Each normalized article contains its canonical URL, contributor/history URL, archive identity and available revision identity. The source archive's publisher is openZIM/Kiwix; that does not replace Wikipedia contributor attribution. Do not reuse images or treat arbitrary linked third-party content as uniformly licensed Wikipedia text.

The optional English travel library contains text from Wikivoyage contributors under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). It uses the September 2026 Kiwix snapshot; article-history links are retained for contributor attribution, and images are excluded. See the [Wikivoyage reuse guide](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wikivoyage:How_to_re-use_Wikivoyage_guides) before reusing the data.

The optional U.S. outdoor-safety library contains text from four National Park Service pages. NPS says material it created is generally in the U.S. public domain unless otherwise marked, but page materials can include third-party rights. This edition excludes images and NPS marks; its lock records the source pages and normalized text hashes. The package and catalog attribute the National Park Service and state that Blobs is independent and not endorsed by NPS. See the [NPS disclaimer and reuse notice](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm).

The optional U.S. emergency-preparedness library contains text from four Ready.gov pages. FEMA says most material on FEMA.gov may be copied and distributed, but also notes that some material may be licensed or restricted. FEMA identifies Ready.gov as a site it owns and manages. The edition excludes images, agency marks, and linked third-party resources; its source lock records canonical pages, update dates, and normalized-text digests. The package and catalog identify the U.S. scope, tell users to follow current local emergency instructions, and state that Blobs is independent and not endorsed by FEMA. See [FEMA website information](https://www.fema.gov/about/website-information) and [USAGov's federal-material copyright guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright).
