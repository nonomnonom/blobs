# Corpus preparation and data notices

`libzim` 3.13.1 is a host-side Python binding to libzim, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. Its installed license is preserved in [licenses/python-libzim-GPL-3.0.txt](licenses/python-libzim-GPL-3.0.txt). Source distributions, wheel hashes and versions are pinned in `uv.lock`; upstream source is [openzim/python-libzim](https://github.com/openzim/python-libzim). The APK does not embed this dependency. Redistribution of preparation tooling must retain applicable GPL obligations; this does not assign an owner-selected license to Blobs' original application code.

`selectolax` 1.0.0 uses the Lexbor HTML parser. Its MIT notice is preserved in [licenses/selectolax-MIT.txt](licenses/selectolax-MIT.txt). See [selectolax](https://github.com/rushter/selectolax) and [Lexbor](https://github.com/lexbor/lexbor) for upstream source and notices. Development environments and third-party wheels are not included in source bundles; the lock file recreates them.

Wikipedia text remains attributed to Wikipedia contributors under the applicable [CC BY-SA 4.0 text](licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt). Blobs extracts/reformats text, removes page navigation and images, and converts tables to text rows. Each normalized article contains its canonical URL, contributor/history URL, archive identity and available revision identity. The source archive's publisher is openZIM/Kiwix; that does not replace Wikipedia contributor attribution. Do not reuse images or treat arbitrary linked third-party content as uniformly licensed Wikipedia text.

The optional English travel library contains text from Wikivoyage contributors under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). It uses the September 2026 Kiwix snapshot; article-history links are retained for contributor attribution, and images are excluded. See the [Wikivoyage reuse guide](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Wikivoyage:How_to_re-use_Wikivoyage_guides) before reusing the data.

The Bali travel library selects 30 articles from that same reviewed Wikivoyage edition. It retains each article's text, contributor credit, history URL and original snapshot provenance under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its selection lock pins the input record manifest and every selected article's identity, URL and text hash. It contains text guides to places and transport, without map tiles or route calculation.

The practical-living library contains four Appropedia articles: Composting, Drip irrigation, Rainwater harvesting and Solar cooker. Although Appropedia's general footer names CC BY-SA 4.0, each included article specifies **CC BY-SA 3.0**. The pack preserves that article-specific license, source URLs and contributor-history links. The [license text](licenses/CC-BY-SA-3.0.txt) is included in this repository and the Android app's offline notices. Blobs pins individual revisions, removes navigation, images and embedded media, and reformats the remaining text. External linked resources and hardware designs are not included. See [Appropedia's intellectual-property policy](https://www.appropedia.org/Appropedia:Intellectual_property). This community reference collection does not imply Appropedia's endorsement.

The optional U.S. outdoor-safety library contains text from five National Park Service pages. NPS says material it created is generally in the U.S. public domain unless otherwise marked, but page materials can include third-party rights. This edition excludes images and NPS marks; its lock records the source pages and normalized text hashes. The package and catalog attribute the National Park Service and state that Blobs is independent and not endorsed by NPS. See the [NPS disclaimer and reuse notice](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm).

The optional camping-safety library contains text from four NPS pages in its camping-safety series. It excludes images and linked content and preserves source URLs and update dates; users are directed to current park rules and ranger instructions because these pages were last updated in 2017–2019.

The optional backcountry-planning package contains three NPS articles: Ten Essentials, Trip Planning Guide, and Wilderness & Backcountry Camping. NPS material is generally public domain unless otherwise marked; the Ten Essentials page credits The Mountaineers, and that credit is retained in the extracted text. Images and agency marks are excluded. Source attribution and the notice that Blobs is independent and not endorsed by NPS remain with the package and catalog. See the [NPS disclaimer and reuse notice](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm).

The U.S. emergency-preparedness, weather-safety, earthquake/tsunami-safety, and emergency-food libraries contain text from Ready.gov. FEMA says most material on FEMA.gov may be copied and distributed, but also notes that some material may be licensed or restricted. FEMA identifies Ready.gov as a site it owns and manages. These editions exclude images, agency marks, and linked third-party resources; their source locks record canonical pages, update dates, and normalized-text digests. The packages and catalog identify the U.S. scope, tell users to follow current local emergency instructions, and state that Blobs is independent and not endorsed by FEMA. See [FEMA website information](https://www.fema.gov/about/website-information) and [USAGov's federal-material copyright guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright).

The original Android contract-test article is explicitly CC0-1.0 and contains imaginary measurements. It is not included in the knowledge pack. See [Android notices](../android/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for runtime, model and application ownership.
