# Community conduct

Help keep Blobs useful and welcoming for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-managed spaces.

- Discuss the code, evidence and user experience respectfully. Explain disagreement and offer actionable feedback.
- Allow questions and mistakes. Avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language and threats.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal information or private communications without permission.
- Keep conversations relevant and give contributors room to participate.

The repository owner moderates project spaces and may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or block repeated or serious abuse. Moderation should consider the context and explain decisions when doing so does not expose private information or create further harm.

For private abuse reports, use GitHub's **Report content** control on the relevant content or [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). These reports go through GitHub's platform reporting process. Do not put sensitive personal details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting channel](SECURITY.md).
